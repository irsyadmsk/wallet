# Product context

**Wallet** is a personal and family money ledger (Flutter: web at
wallet.irsyads.com and an Android APK). Built for Muhammad Irsyad Sirojul
Khoeir — a sociology teacher and vice principal in Purwakarta — and his
household.

- **Who:** Indonesian-speaking adults managing a household budget together;
  some are not finance-savvy. Used on phones during the day and on a laptop
  when reviewing the month.
- **Jobs:** record spending in seconds; see where money went and what to do
  about it; keep one shared ledger with family members in sync; handle
  zakat correctly.
- **Constraints:** sign-in required; data syncs through Supabase with row
  level security; works offline and syncs later; Indonesian UI (Bahasa
  Indonesia baku), rupiah formatting, legal references cited by number
  (UU/PMA) where the app gives religious or tax guidance.
- **Mode:** Operate for the app, Persuade for the welcome screen.
- **Tone:** calm, factual, inductive — observations first, conclusions after.
